# FDA ST&F requirement matrix

Source of record for the figures of the FDA *Standard Safety Tables and Figures: Integrated Guide*, version 2.0 (April 2025; CDER BIRRS, MAPP 6025.9, [PDF](https://www.fda.gov/media/187065/download)) as gsm.safety implements them, and for the cross-cutting rules the guide states once and every display reads. The shape is the safety.viz matrix shape ([safety.viz `requirements/`](https://github.com/jwildfire/safety.viz/tree/dev/requirements)), so one requirement ID can be cited by a static rendering in this package and by an interactive rendering in safety.viz, and the two evidence sets meet on the same row. How rows are keyed and how a test cites one is in [README.md](README.md).

Every row names the guide version and the section it comes from. Figures carry the chart engine that renders them (from the [FDA ST&F static display strategy](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/reports/fda-stf-static-displays-plan-2026-07-21/) and its [inventory](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/reports/fda-stf-static-displays-plan-2026-07-21/fda_stf_inventory.json)), the ADaM domains they read, the safety.viz module that is their interactive twin where one exists, and the phase of the [design for requirement #9](https://jwildfire.github.io/obot.roadmap/requirements/design/9_design.html) that ships them. Rules carry the `Derive_*` function or renderer convention that implements them.

## Source inventory

- FDA ST&F Integrated Guide v2.0, sections 2 (Core), 3 (Expanded), 4 (Optional) and 5 (Appendix): figure example pages and their notes, the background and customization text of each figure's section, and Appendix Tables 56 to 60.
- `fda_stf_inventory.json` (obot.roadmap, 2026-07-21): the 22 figures with tier, section, chart type and domains, and the cross-cutting rules harvested from the guide.
- Design for requirement #9 (2026-07-21, rescoped 2026-09-11): the engines, the phases, and the L1 contract that the `FDA-RULE-*` rows implement.
